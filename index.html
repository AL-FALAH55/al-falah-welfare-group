Membership & Guidelines

Membership Contribution & Emergency Assistance

Joining Fee: ₹100

One-time fee · Non-refundable

Weekly Association Contribution: ₹100

Required while a member remains enrolled, whether or not a member takes a loan.

Emergency Interest-Free Assistance: ₹1,000 – ₹20,000

No collateral · Subject to available funds and committee approval

  • Weekly membership contribution is separate from any loan repayment.
  • Loan repayment amounts and terms are set according to the association's schedule.
  • Accounts and payments are recorded transparently.
Member conduct

Discipline · 8 Rules

  • Good character: Responsible, hardworking and respectful conduct.
  • No intoxication: Membership is not for those involved in intoxicant abuse.
  • No gambling: Gambling and betting activities are prohibited.
  • No disputes or abuse: Threats, violence, abusive language and misconduct are prohibited.
  • Hard work: Give priority to positive work and self-improvement.
  • Discipline: Respect committee decisions and association rules.
  • Accountability: Complaints will be reviewed; warning, suspension or termination may follow fair review.
  • Purpose: Encourage honesty, effort, discipline, unity and mutual respect.
